What is Zakat?

The third pillar of Islam — purification of wealth and soul. Due when qualifying wealth stays above nisab for one lunar year.

The Five Pillars

  1. Shahada (Faith)
  2. Salah (Prayer)
  3. Zakat (Charity)
  4. Sawm (Fasting)
  5. Hajj (Pilgrimage)

Zakat rate — 2.5%

2.5% of qualifying wealth held for one lunar year (hawl).

Nisab threshold (09/10/2026)

  • Silver: 612.36g = £896.93 (default)
  • Gold: 87.48g = £8,885.68

Who can receive (Qur'an 9:60)

  • The poor and needy
  • Those in debt
  • Travellers in difficulty
  • Causes in the path of Allah
  • New Muslims and those in captivity

Zakatable wealth

  • Gold and silver at market value
  • Cash, savings, ISAs
  • Trade stock and inventory
  • Shares, funds, crypto
  • Investment property (home exempt)
  • Accessible pensions — opinions differ, ask a scholar
£10,000 net assets → £250 Zakat (2.5%).
